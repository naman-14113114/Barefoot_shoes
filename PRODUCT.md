# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

UK shoppers comparing premium-looking barefoot footwear on desktop, tablet, and mobile. They need to understand the shoe, choose a colour and size, and reach checkout without friction.

## Product Purpose

BUUDY is a multi-country barefoot-footwear storefront. The UK product page should combine the visual restraint and interaction quality of ETQ with BUUDY's own identity, barefoot positioning, product information, pricing, and commerce flow.

## Brand Personality

Precise, restrained, and confident. The experience should feel like a carefully art-directed premium footwear store: quiet typography, disciplined spacing, product-led imagery, and motion that supports navigation rather than calling attention to itself.

## Anti-references

Avoid crowded promotional stacks, oversized mobile typography, generic modal-heavy ecommerce UI, decorative animation, broken tablet breakpoints, and controls that expose implementation details. Do not overwrite or dilute the customised barefoot product information below the gallery.

## Design Principles

- Put the product imagery and purchase decision first.
- Match the approved ETQ reference in proportion, hierarchy, interaction, and responsive behaviour while retaining BUUDY identity.
- Treat tablet and mobile as intentionally composed experiences, not compressed desktop layouts.
- Keep commerce controls immediate, legible, keyboard-accessible, and free of tracking or animation delays.
- Preserve the existing barefoot-specific product content and underlying checkout behaviour.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible focus states, Escape and backdrop dismissal for overlays, keyboard-operable galleries and menus, 44px touch targets where practical, body-scroll locking for open drawers, useful alternative text, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
